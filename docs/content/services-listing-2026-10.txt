[Heading3] AI Ideation Workshop (AI Lab)
The AI Lab is a facilitated workshop where your business and technology leaders discover, shape and prioritize AI opportunities for your own organization. Your team moves from awareness to a shortlist of validated use cases and a plan for the first pilot.
What we deliver
[ListParagraph] • AI awareness session for executives and teams

[ListParagraph] • Outline of a prototype or proof of concept
[ListParagraph] • Pilot plan with owners and success measures
AI governance built into the methodology
Governance is part of how we take each use case from idea to live operation. It is built into the implementation methodology from the first step, so every use case passes through eight checks in order.
[ListParagraph] • Use case classification. We classify each use case by its purpose and its level of risk.
[ListParagraph] • Data assessment. We confirm the data is available, of sufficient quality and approved for this use.
[ListParagraph] • Clinical risk. For healthcare use cases, we assess the potential impact on patient safety and care decisions.
[ListParagraph] • Privacy. We check how personal data is used and protected, in line with applicable regulations.
[ListParagraph] • Cybersecurity. We define the security controls needed around the model, the data and the integrations.
[ListParagraph] • Human oversight. We agree who reviews AI outputs and who remains accountable for decisions.
[ListParagraph] • Validation. We test accuracy, reliability and fairness before go-live.
[ListParagraph] • Monitoring. We track performance after go-live and act when results drift.
The outcome: a shortlist of AI use cases your own team believes in, and a pilot that is ready to start.

[Heading2] Coverage Models

Coverage model
How it works
Full Managed Services
Grow Digital operates and supports the entire IT environment and is accountable for all service levels.
Partial Managed Services
Grow Digital is responsible for selected systems or service areas. Your IT team or other vendors keep the rest.
Co-managed Services
Responsibility is shared within the same systems. For example, Grow Digital provides specialist or after-hours support behind your own service desk.

Dedicated Managed Services

Shared Managed Services

On-Demand Support

Hybrid Model
A combination of dedicated and shared services for cost efficiency.
