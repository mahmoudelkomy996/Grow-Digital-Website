One emerging trend that has gained traction is the concept of one-stop-shop retail, where consumers can find a diverse range of products and services under a single roof. 1. The One-Stop Shop Retail Trend: Consumers in the Gulf region are increasingly seeking convenience and efficiency in their shopping experiences. They prefer destinations that offer a wide array of products and services in a single location. One-stop shop retail fulfills this demand by providing a seamless and comprehensive shopping experience, saving customers time and effort. It allows retailers to cater to diverse customer needs and increase customer loyalty by becoming a go-to destination for various requirements.
